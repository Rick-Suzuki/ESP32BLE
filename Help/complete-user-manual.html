2. Connect to Mac

2.1. Overview

ESP connects to ESPMacHelper over your local network.

2.2. Install and Launch

  1. Download ESPMacHelper and move it to your Mac's Applications folder.
  2. Open ESPMacHelper.
  3. Look for the cyan E icon in the Mac menu bar.
  4. Click the icon and choose “Show Status Window”.

ESPMacHelper starts automatically when opened.

2.3. Allow Accessibility

ESPMacHelper needs Accessibility permission to send keyboard commands to your Mac.

Open System Settings, find Accessibility, and enable ESPMacHelper.

Open Show Status Window and check that Accessibility says Allowed.

2.4. Connection

Open ESP on your iPhone or iPad.

Connect to your Mac using ESP's Mac connection controls.

Complete pairing if requested.

2.5. Test Connection

Open a text editor on your Mac.

Press the Set & Test button.

Check that the following text appears in your editor:

Hello World! ABC abc 123 456 aa BB 11 !! ?? -- ..

4. Connect Your iOS Device to ESP32BLE

4.1. Overview

Open ESP Settings and select ESP32 mode.

ESP starts looking for available ESP32 devices.

4.2. Find Your ESP32

ESP lists discovered ESP32 devices in Settings.

Wait for your ESP32 to appear.

4.3. Connect

Select your discovered ESP32 in Settings.

Settings shows when ESP32 is connected.

4.4. Test Keyboard

Open a text editor on the device connected to the ESP32-S3 USB keyboard port.

Press Set & Test in ESP Settings.

Check that the following text appears:

Hello World! ABC abc 123 456 aa BB 11 !! ?? -- ..

4.5. Reconnection and Problems

Return to Settings to review the ESP32 connection status.

If your device is not listed, ESP continues searching for available ESP32 devices.

5.1.1. Overview

Get familiar with the ESP main screen, programmable buttons, and switching between screens.

5.1.2. ESP Main Screen

ESP uses a programmable button grid with toolbars for screens, editing, and controls.

6. Troubleshooting

6.1. ESP32 Not Found

Check that the ESP32 is connected to power and is running compatible firmware.

Turn on Bluetooth on your iPhone or iPad, select ESP32 output mode, and look in ESP Settings. Do not look in iOS Bluetooth Settings.

6.2. ESP32 Won't Connect

Check that you selected the intended device and that it is still available and powered.

Allow ESP to scan again, then try selecting the device from the refreshed list.

6.3. Keyboard Not Working

Confirm the active output mode and its connection. For ESP32 mode, check the ESP32 USB HID connection to the target device.

Use Set & Test with a text-entry field where appropriate. Mac mode sends output through ESPMacHelper instead of the ESP32 USB keyboard connection.

6.4. Mac Not Connecting

Make sure ESPMacHelper is running and that your iPhone or iPad and Mac can communicate over the local network.

Select Mac output mode, open the ESPMacHelper status window, and confirm its relevant macOS permissions.

6.5. Buttons Not Responding

Confirm that the intended screen is selected and that the button has an action.

Check the active output mode and connection status, then try a simple keyboard action.
