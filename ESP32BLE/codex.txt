	Remaining limitation
	
	If the authenticated connection is lost or replaced after a Note On, the app intentionally does not send that old session’s Note Off into the new session. Local preview state is cleared, but a disconnected receiver may retain a sounding note according to its own MIDI/disconnection behaviour.
