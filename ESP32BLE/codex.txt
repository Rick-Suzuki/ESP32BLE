4. Duplicate service discovery

didConnect inserts the peripheral UUID into serviceDiscoveryPeripheralIDs.

Only the first insertion calls discoverServices(...). Later callbacks produce:

[BLE-CONNECT-PROBE] SKIPPED DUPLICATE SERVICE DISCOVERY

Probe-only characteristic discovery that completed before selection is explicitly allowed to rediscover for selected-device initialization.

5. Duplicate characteristic discovery

didDiscoverServices inserts the UUID into characteristicDiscoveryPeripheralIDs.

Only the first insertion calls discoverCharacteristics(...). Repeated service callbacks produce:

[BLE-CONNECT-PROBE] SKIPPED DUPLICATE CHARACTERISTIC DISCOVERY

6. Selected initialization protection

selectedInitializationPeripheralIDs allows selected initialization once per connection lifecycle.

Duplicate characteristic callbacks no longer repeat:

RX/TX assignment
TX notification setup
DEVICE_ID reads
connectionText = "Connected"
sendStoredKeyboardTiming()

Suppressed callbacks produce:

[BLE-CONNECT-PROBE] SKIPPED DUPLICATE SELECTED INITIALIZATION

Probe-only characteristic discovery does not mark selected initialization complete.

7. Tracking lifecycle and retries

Per-peripheral tracking is cleared by:

didDisconnectPeripheral
didFailToConnect

All tracking is cleared by stop() because that deliberately ends all managed connections.

Service and characteristic discovery failures release their request tracking without introducing an automatic retry loop. Missing service or characteristic results also release the relevant request tracking.

Other peripherals’ tracking is unaffected by a single peripheral disconnect or failure.
