5. Completed keyboard combinations

When a non-modifier key follows pending modifiers, the executor sends:

pending modifiers + current key

in one call to sendKeyboardOutputTokens at lines 569-580.

For example:

["ct", "cm", "f19"]

now reaches MacConnectionManager together, producing:

CTRL+CMD+F19

The pending modifiers are cleared immediately after transmission.
