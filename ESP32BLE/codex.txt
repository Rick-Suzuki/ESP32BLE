ESP32 toggles ESP32 <-> Off.

Mac discovery/connection stops.

LIVE BLE DISCOVERY

Root cause:
BLEDiscoveredDevice already stored lastSeenAt, RSSI, deviceID, and peripheral identity.
BLEKeyboardManager already had discoveredDeviceTimeout, staleDeviceCleanupInterval, and removeStaleDiscoveredDevices().
But startStaleDeviceCleanupTimer() only invalidated the timer and never scheduled a new one.
So CoreBluetooth discoveries were effectively additive during continuous scanning, and stale devices stayed in discoveredDevices until a fresh scan cleared the list.

Fix:
startScan() now starts a lightweight cleanup timer.
The timer runs every 2 seconds.
Devices not seen for 8 seconds are removed unless they are the actively connected ESP32.
If the removed device was selected, selectedPeripheralID is cleared.
Returning ESP32s are restored by the existing didDiscover path when advertisements resume.
