C. Gesture changes

Screen rows now use the same exclusive gesture arrangement as Images:

• Double tap: TapGesture(count: 2)
• Single tap: TapGesture(count: 1)
• The double gesture is exclusive before the single gesture.

This ensures only one action executes:


• Single tap copies the full original filename and shows “copied name.”
• Double tap loads the Screen and shows “changed screen.”
