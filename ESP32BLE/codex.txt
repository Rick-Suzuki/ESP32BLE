Conflict behavior:

• Replace All imports everything and preserves the existing replacement mechanism for this phase.
• Skip All skips conflicting destination filenames.
• If a skipped file is a .screen, its matching .config.json from the backup is also skipped.

• Cancel clears pending restore state and removes temporary extraction directories without importing.
