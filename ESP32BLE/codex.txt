C. New parser grammar

Implemented at ESP32BLE/Scrns/MIDI/MIDIControlChangeParser.swift:145-203.

One-shot:
.cc <type> <start> [channel]

Hold:
.cc <type> <start> <end> [channel] hold

Toggle:
.cc <type> <start> <end> [channel] toggle

• Start and End accept 0...127.
• Optional channel accepts 1...16.
• Omitted channel remains nil for existing configured-default resolution.
• A final hold or toggle determines the positional grammar.
• Unexpected argument counts are rejected.
