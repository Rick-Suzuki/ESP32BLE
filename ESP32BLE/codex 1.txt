A. VIEWER ROOT CAUSE

The iPhone Plates viewer is still routed and instantiated correctly:

• iPhoneLargeContentPreview selects iPhoneTemplateViewerSection when Templates is active: Set_Screen.swift:3133-3142.
• isIPhoneContentPreviewPresented includes the Templates visibility flag: Set_Screen.swift:3145-3149.
• Selecting a template sets isIPhoneTemplatePreviewPresented = true: Set_Screen.swift:3861-3871.

There is no source evidence that the viewer was removed or that its visibility flag is cleared while Templates remains active.

The regression is layout-related. iPhoneImageEditorBody gives the viewer the width measured from iPhoneBottomControls: Set_AvailableDevices.swift:116-136. Stage E.2 added the full navigation group and slider to that same bottom controls row: Set_AvailableDevices.swift:160-171.

That row is therefore both:

• The width-measurement source for the viewer.
• A new host for a large navigation-controls hierarchy.

The panel also shares the iPhone bottom Settings area with the always-present keyboard-settings column in combinedBottomPanelSection: Set_Screen.swift:3697-3729. The enlarged bottom row can force the preview-host column to be compressed, including to an unusable width. This is the source-level cause consistent with the missing viewer.

The viewer is not blocked by preview routing; it is being displaced/compressed by the added bottom-row controls.
