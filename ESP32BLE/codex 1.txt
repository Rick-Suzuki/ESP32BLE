• Message: Some files already exist and will be overwritten. Do you want to continue?

L. Remaining limitation/risk

The batch is fully validated before publication, and replacements are individually staged atomically. A later copy failure can still leave earlier non-conflicting files imported; it is reported as partial failure and never treated as a complete success. Full cross-file transactional rollback is not present in the existing importer architecture and was not introduced in this narrow stage.
